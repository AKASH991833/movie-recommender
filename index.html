CineMatch

Movies you'll love, explained

Rate a handful of movies and CineMatch builds a taste profile from your ratings - then recommends from 280 top-rated films on the real MovieLens dataset, telling you exactly why.

Honest limits. Recommendations come from a catalog of the 280 highest-rated films in the MovieLens (latest-small, 100k ratings) research dataset using content-based matching - no collaborative filtering across users, no posters (rights), and the catalog stops at 2018. Your ratings never leave this device.
